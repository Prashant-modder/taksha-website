Delete Account

You can delete your MeritBits account at any time. Here's exactly how, and what happens to your data.

Overview Features Screenshots Download Privacy Terms Delete Account

MeritBits is published by Taksha. This page explains how to delete your MeritBits account and what happens to the data associated with it. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open MeritBits on your Android device.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with your account password.

Class teachers: before deleting your account you must first delete or hand over any classes you own. A class can only be deleted once its students have been removed.

Deletion takes effect immediately. There is no waiting period.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email (if you cannot log in)

If you can no longer access your account — for example, you've lost access to the email address or forgotten your password — you can request manual deletion:

  1. Send an email to zirconium4.1@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: MeritBits account deletion request.
  3. Send it from the email address associated with your MeritBits account if possible.
  4. Include your name and role (Teacher or Parent).

Manual deletion requests are processed as soon as possible — typically within a few days. We may ask a confirmation question to verify ownership before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record — name, email, password, role, approval status
  • For parents: the links to your children in your account
  • For teachers: your teacher record, approval request, and membership in any classes you joined
  • Any profile image you uploaded

What is not deleted

Some data belongs to the classroom, not to your account. It stays because the school's tracking record must remain intact:

  • Student records — name, roll number, class, merit, and full history remain in the classroom. Deleting a parent account never deletes a student record.
  • Thoughts you posted — remain visible in the class feed with your name attached. This is disclosed openly so you can decide before posting.

Parent accounts may retain a stale reference to a student that has since been deleted by their teacher. This reference is skipped silently by the app and does not expose any data.

What about students?

Students never have accounts. Only a class teacher can delete a student. When a teacher deletes a student, the student's record, merit, and full history are permanently deleted — there is no archive.

Data retention after deletion

Account deletion is immediate. Once your account is deleted, we do not retain a copy for any period. Because MeritBits uses Google Firebase, minor residual backups may exist briefly within Google's infrastructure as part of normal service operation, but they are not accessible to Taksha and expire automatically.

Questions

Anything unclear? Email zirconium4.1@gmail.com before deleting, and we'll help.
