About Taksha

A small software studio with a simple belief: apps should respect the people who use them.

01 — The name

Where "Taksha" comes from

The name Taksha was chosen for its Sanskrit and Devanagari-inspired feel. It carries an echo of Takshashila — the ancient center of learning that drew students from across the world. Not a direct claim to that legacy, but a nod to what it stood for: knowledge, discipline, and building things that last.

It also happened to have a vibe adjacent to a name I loved — Voksha — and once the sound clicked, it stuck. Sometimes the right name is the one that already feels like yours.

02 — The studio

Separate from Cryonix Labs

Taksha is its own home for software — intentionally kept distinct from Cryonix Labs. Different projects, different focus, different audiences. Taksha is where apps live that are meant to feel calm, compressed, and quietly powerful.

Every product under Taksha shares the same principles: no ads, no trackers, no dark patterns. Just software that does its job and stays out of the way.

03 — What we're building

MeritBits, and what comes after

The first flagship product is MeritBits — a digital classroom credit and behaviour-tracking system built around accountability, transparency, and student growth. It's currently in development, and you'll know when it's ready.

After MeritBits, more is planned. Taksha isn't a one-app studio. It's a long-term bet on the kind of software that quietly earns your trust.

04 — The principles

What we stand for

  • No ads. Ever. Not now, not later, not hidden in a corner.
  • Compressed & clean. Small downloads. Every feature earns its place.
  • Few, honest premiums. A handful of fair paid options — nothing more.
  • Built to last. Apps that still feel right in five years.
