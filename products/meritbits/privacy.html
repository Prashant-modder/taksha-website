Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 3, 2026

Overview Features Download Privacy Terms Delete Account

MeritBits is a classroom credit and behaviour-tracking app published by Taksha. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, how it is stored, and the choices you have. It applies to the MeritBits Android application (com.taksha.meritbits) and to the website at taksha.pro.

1. Who can use MeritBits

MeritBits is designed for teachers and parents. Accounts may only be created by people aged 18 or older. The app does not knowingly allow users under 18 to register. Students do not create accounts, do not log in, and do not interact with the app directly. Student data is entered by teachers for classroom tracking purposes.

If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us at zirconium4.1@gmail.com and we will remove it.

2. What we collect

MeritBits collects the minimum data needed to run a classroom merit system.

Account data (teachers and parents)

  • Your name, as entered at registration
  • Your email address, used for login, verification, and password reset
  • A hashed password (we never see the plain password)
  • Your role (Teacher or Parent) and, for teachers, your approval status
  • For parents: an internal list of child IDs you have linked
  • An optional profile image if you choose to upload one

Student data (entered by teachers)

  • Student name
  • Roll number
  • Class the student belongs to
  • Current merit value
  • History — each entry contains reason, points, and a server timestamp
  • A private link code used inside the student's QR, which can be rotated at any time

Classroom data

  • Class name, join code, and list of teachers with their roles
  • Thoughts posted by teachers — message text, author name, and timestamp

3. What we do not collect

MeritBits contains no ads, no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites. We do not sell or rent any data.

MeritBits uses Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, and Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) — Google services necessary for login and database security. See Firebase's own privacy information for how Google handles authentication.

4. Notifications

The app may send local notifications to parents when a child's merit changes or a new thought is posted. Notifications are generated locally on your device — no server-side push service is used. The app performs periodic background checks (approximately every 15 minutes, via Android WorkManager) to look for updates while the app is closed. If the app is open, changes appear almost immediately. Timings may vary under battery saver mode. You can disable notifications at any time in Android settings.

5. Where your data is stored

MeritBits stores classroom data in Firebase Realtime Database, region asia-southeast1 (Singapore). Authentication data is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. All communication between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit (TLS).

6. How access is controlled

  • Teachers can only read students in classes they belong to.
  • Parents can only read students they have linked via the student's QR code.
  • Subject teachers can lower merit but cannot raise it.
  • Only class teachers can raise merit via the reset action.
  • History entries cannot be edited once written.
  • Firebase App Check with Play Integrity blocks requests from non-genuine app builds.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account inside the app. Deletion is immediate and requires your password. Full details are on the Account Deletion page.

If you can no longer log in, email zirconium4.1@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and we will delete it manually
