// MeritBits · Features

What's inside.

Everything a classroom needs to track behaviour, keep parents informed, and stay transparent.

Overview Features Download Privacy Terms Delete Account
◈

Digital Merit balance

Every student has a live merit value that starts at 5000. Negative balances are shown in red and carry into the next reset — no quiet floor, no fudging.

◆

One-tap deductions

Teachers pick an infraction from the deduction table, confirm, and points are subtracted instantly. History is written automatically with reason and timestamp.

◇

Full history log

Every change is saved. View as a list or as a graph. History entries cannot be edited once written — what's recorded is what stays.

⬡

Parent view with notifications

Parents see their child's merit out of 5000, progress bar, and history. When merit changes or a new thought is posted, a local notification appears on the phone — while the app is open almost immediately, and via periodic background checks otherwise.

⬢

QR-based linking

Each student has a private QR. A parent scans it once to link the child. The QR can be rotated at any time, and old codes stop working immediately.

✧

Thoughts feed

Teachers post short messages (up to 500 characters) to a class. Parents read them. Admins moderate. A quiet channel between classroom and home.

Also inside

Smaller things that matter.

  • Class & subject teachers — separate permissions with a 6-character join code
  • Bulk student add — paste a list, invalid lines are flagged individually
  • Admin panel — approve or reject teacher requests, moderate thoughts
  • Themes — Dark, Royal (nine backgrounds + nine accents), and Soft Light
  • Offline documentation — the Beta and 1.0 PDF guides bundle inside the app
  • Email verification and admin approval for teachers, enforced for new accounts
  • Offline banner — a red bar when the device has no connection
Data & safety

Locked down by rules.

Database access is enforced on the server. Teachers can only read students in their own classes. Parents can only read linked children. Merit writes are restricted to the right roles — subject teachers can lower merit but never raise it. History entries are immutable.

Firebase App Check with Play Integrity protects the database from non-genuine apps. No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads. Notifications are local to the device.

Privacy, terms, and account deletion are all one click away.
