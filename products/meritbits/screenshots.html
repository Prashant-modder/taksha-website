Teacher dashboard

Your class at a glance.

See every student, their roll number, and current merit — all in one view. Tap any student card to open their panel. The + button adds a student. Reset All Merit is always one tap away.

History & deductions

Every change, logged forever.

Each deduction writes a permanent entry — reason, points, and timestamp. Parents receive a local notification the moment merit changes. The history view can render as a list or a graph.

Thoughts & communication

A quiet channel between classroom and home.

Teachers post short messages to a class. Parents read them in a clean, distraction-free feed. No replies, no notifications war — just context.

Sign in & onboarding

Simple, calm, unmistakably MeritBits.

The login screen greets you with a friendly bear — smiling when ready, covering its eyes while you type your password. No pressure, no dark patterns.

Profile & settings

Yours to shape.

Profile shows your role, email, member-since date, and class count. Settings opens up theming — Dark, Royal, and Soft Light, with nine background colours and nine accent colours to combine freely. You can even change the app icon.

Notes

Beta screens may change before public launch.

MeritBits is currently in closed testing. Screens shown reflect version 1.0.0 (build 1) running on Android with the Royal Ruby theme. Colours, spacing, and details may shift slightly between builds.
